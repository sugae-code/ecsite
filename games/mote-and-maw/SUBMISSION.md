## Publisher-facing metadata (English)

**Title:** Mote & Maw
**Tagline:** Grow from a tiny glow to a radiant survivor in a one-finger arcade chase.
**Genre:** Arcade / skill / survival.
**Language:** English.

**Description:** Steer a curious glowing mote around a living field of lights. Absorb smaller sparks to grow, outmaneuver larger hunters, chain combos, and use a powerful pulse when things get close. Keep evolving until you bloom. Original canvas graphics and synthesized effects; works with mouse, touch, and keyboard.

**Controls:** Move using mouse, touch, WASD or arrow keys. Press SPACE or PULSE to repel hunters at the expense of 13% of your size. Restart using ENTER or the on-screen button. Sound is optional.

## Upload and approval requirements

- Upload `index.html`, `style.css`, `engine.mjs`, `game.mjs`, `crazygames-bridge.mjs` as a root-level static HTML5 build.
- Three required cover art ratios: landscape, square, portrait. Two short preview videos. These binaries are prepared separately in the existing owner-supplied submission ZIP, not in this repository.
- HTML5 SDK v3 loads only on CrazyGames and localhost, and falls back safely on standalone hosts. Gameplay lifecycle and host mute settings are implemented.
- `CRAZYGAMES_FULL_LAUNCH_APPROVED` remains `false`. The SDK ad hook is deliberately not enabled before actual Full Launch acceptance.
- Basic Launch = evaluation only, **no ad revenue**. Full Launch = review/SDK integration required before revshare.
- Developer portal submission and ownership/payout authentication have not been performed by the assistant. Do not claim accepted, contracted, earning or paid.
- The current publisher terms specify a €100 minimum balance before CrazyGames payments.
